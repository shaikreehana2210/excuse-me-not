# Excuse Roaster

Create a simple, cute, playful and highly decorative web app called Excuse.exe 💀📚



Concept



A student types an excuse for not studying.



The app responds with a funny, friendly AI roast and then gives them a tiny study challenge.



The personality should feel like a funny best friend teasing you, NOT like a corporate productivity app.



---



🎀 Visual Style



Make the entire website adorable and visually memorable.



Use:



- Soft pastel colors

- Cute gradients

- Rounded cards

- Puffy/bubble-style buttons

- Doodles

- Stars ✨

- Hearts 💗

- Sparkles ✨

- Tiny books 📚

- Coffee cups ☕

- Pencils ✏️

- Cute clouds ☁️

- Sticky-note style cards

- Small decorative stickers

- Playful micro-animations



Use a clean white/very-light pastel background with colorful decorative elements.



Keep everything spacious and readable.



Do NOT make it cluttered.



The design should feel like:

Pinterest + cute study desk + Gen-Z web app.



---



🏠 Home Page



At the top:



Excuse.exe 💀



Small subtitle:



"Your excuses have been reported to the study department."



Hero heading:



"Why didn't you study? 👀"



Subtitle:



"Tell me your excuse. I promise I'll only judge a little."



Add a cute illustrated study desk decoration beside the input.



Large rounded text box:



"I didn't study because..."



Button:



"Roast Me 🔥"



Add tiny decorative text underneath:



"Don't worry. It'll hurt only a little."



---



🔥 Roast Screen



When the user submits an excuse, show a cute loading animation:



"Analyzing your academic crime..."

📚 🔍 💀



Then reveal the roast inside a large decorative card.



Heading:



"OH NO... 💀"



Display the AI roast in large text.



Example:



User:

"I was tired."



AI:



«"Tired? Bestie, your textbook has been waiting longer than your motivation. 😭📚"»



Add cute floating emojis around the card.



---



🎯 Excuse Score



Show a cute circular score:



87/100

"Professional Procrastinator 🏆"

- Professional Procrastinator

- Certified Tomorrow Person

- Last-Minute Legend

- Actually Trying 👏



---



💌 Reality Check



Add a cute sticky-note card titled:



"Okay... but seriously 🥺"



Give one short constructive sentence.



Example:



"You don't need to study for 5 hours. Just start with 15 minutes."



---



📚 Tiny Study Challenge



Show a cute card:



"Your punishment... I mean, challenge 😇"



Example:



Study for 15 minutes.



Then button:



"Okay, I'll do it 🫡"

Create a simple functional timer.



Show a large:

Start

Pause

Reset



When completed:



"YOU ACTUALLY STUDIED?! 😭🎉"



Subtitle:



"Character development unlocked."



Add a cute celebration animation.

4

"You're doing better than yesterday 💗"



---



😂 Roast Again



After every roast:



"I have another question..."



Button:



"Give Me Another Roast 💀"



Return to the excuse input.



---



🎀 Decorative Details



Add subtle animations:



- Floating stars

- Sparkles

- Bouncing emoji

- Button hover animations

- Card hover effects

- Cute loading animation

- Confetti after completing a study session



Use decorations carefully so they don't interfere with usability.



---



🤖 AI



Use a secure server-side AI function.



The AI should:



- Roast the excuse humorously

- Keep the roast friendly

- Never attack the person's identity, appearance or protected characteristics

- Focus on the excuse

- Give one short reality check

- Give one small study challenge



Keep AI responses short and entertaining.



---



📱 Mobile First



Make the entire experience beautiful on mobile.



The main screen should fit naturally on a phone without feeling cramped.



---



⭐ Important



Keep the app SIMPLE.



Do not add:



- Complex dashboards

- Complicated navigation

- Excessive forms

- Unnecessary settings

- Corporate-looking charts

- Too many pages



The main experience should be:



Type excuse → Get roasted → Get tiny challenge → Study → Celebrate 🎀



Make it feel like a tiny internet app that students would actually want to share with their friends.



The final result should be cute, decorative, funny, addictive and polished.
